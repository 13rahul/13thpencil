Questions people ask

How do you use AI in creative work?

To explore and produce: drafting variations, generating imagery and motion, versioning content for different channels. The decisions about what is right for the brand stay with people.

Will AI-generated content look like everyone else’s?

It will if nobody edits it. The default output of a model is the average of what it has seen, which is exactly the twelfth answer. Direction, selection and finishing are what make it specific to you.

What is an innovation sprint?

A fixed, short window to take a broad ambition, such as using AI in customer experience, and come out with a few concrete, testable ideas and a clear view of which one to build first.

Can you build tools for our internal team?

Yes. Small, focused tools that automate a repetitive step in a team’s workflow are often the fastest return on an AI investment, and they are a good way to learn what the technology can do in your business.
