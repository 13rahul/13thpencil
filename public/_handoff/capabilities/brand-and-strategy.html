• In plain words

Brand strategy and positioning, in plain words.

Brand strategy decides what a company stands for, who it is for and why anyone should choose it over the obvious alternative. Everything else, the name, the logo, the campaign, the website, is easier and cheaper to get right once that is settled.

Our strategy work starts by reading the category the way a customer meets it: every competitor’s own words, side by side, until the pattern shows. The gap is usually the thing everyone has quietly agreed not to say. From there we build a brand positioning, the structure underneath it (brand architecture, naming, what gets a line and what disappears) and a verbal identity your team can use on a Monday morning.

It suits founders launching something new, businesses that have outgrown how they describe themselves, and teams whose marketing has started to sound like everyone else’s. We work from Bangalore with brands wherever they are.

Questions people ask

What is included in a brand strategy project?

Typically a category and competitor audit, a positioning statement, brand architecture, naming where it is needed, messaging and tone of voice, and a short guide your team can actually use. The exact mix depends on where the business is and what decision the strategy has to support.

Is 13th Pencil a branding agency?

Not in the usual sense. 13th Pencil is a creative and innovation company: strategy, creative studio work, AI and brand experiences under one roof, so the thinking does not get lost in the hand-offs between them.

What is the difference between brand strategy and brand identity?

Strategy is the decision: what you stand for and who it is for. Identity is how that decision looks and sounds: logo, colour, typography, language. Identity built without strategy tends to look good and say nothing in particular.

Do you work with startups and new businesses?

Yes. A new business is often the best moment for strategy, because nothing has to be undone. Beardman Company, one of the brands on our Work page, is a new brand we are building now.
