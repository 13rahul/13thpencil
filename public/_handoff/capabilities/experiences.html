Questions people ask

What is experiential marketing?

Marketing that people take part in rather than watch: a launch, an installation, a pop-up, a stand. Done well, it creates a memory and a story people retell, which travels further than an advert.

What do you design for a product launch or event?

The central idea, the guest journey from arrival to exit, the spatial layout, the key moment and how it is staged, and the brand language that ties it together.

Do you design retail spaces and exhibition stands?

Yes. Both have seconds to do their job, so we design them around a single, clear reason to stop, and make that reason visible from a distance.

What makes an event memorable?

One thing that could not happen anywhere else, placed where it will be found, with the rest of the plan built to lead people to it. Memorable events are usually simpler than forgettable ones.
