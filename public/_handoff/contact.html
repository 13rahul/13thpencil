What happens when you get in touch.

Write to hello@13thpencil.com with whatever you have: a full brief, a rough idea or a problem you cannot quite name yet. If you would rather have some structure, the Start a project page asks eight short questions so the first conversation starts in the right place.

We are based in Bangalore and work across brand strategy, creative studio work, AI and innovation and brand experiences, and most projects draw on more than one.

Questions people ask

What should I include in my first message?

What you are working on, what has to change, and anything you already know about timing or budget. A paragraph is plenty.

Do you meet clients in person in Bangalore?

We are based in Bangalore, so meeting in person is often possible. Most projects also run comfortably over video calls, which is how we work with brands outside the city.

Do I need a finished brief?

No. Helping shape the brief is often the most useful part of the first conversation.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Brand strategy and identity, packaging and websites, art direction and film, AI-assisted production and tools, and brand experiences such as launches, events and retail spaces.
