• In plain words

Why 13th Pencil, in plain words.

Ask twelve people, or one machine, for an idea and you will get twelve versions of the same one. They are not wrong. They are just the place where everyone starts.

13th Pencil is a creative and innovation company in Bangalore, built on the idea that the work worth paying for begins after those first answers: in the brand strategy that finds the gap, the creative that says something only you could say, the use of AI that makes room for judgement instead of replacing it, and the brand experiences people remember.

Twelve is a pattern. Thirteen is a decision. Every project we take on is held to that line.

Questions people ask

What does the name 13th Pencil mean?

Twelve pencils make a set; every box has the same twelve. The thirteenth is the one that was not expected. It stands for the idea that only arrives once the obvious ones have been said out loud and set aside.

What kind of company is 13th Pencil?

A creative and innovation company working across four areas: brand strategy, creative studio work, AI and innovation, and brand experiences.

How is that different from a traditional creative agency?

Strategy, creative, technology and experiences are usually separate teams, often separate companies. Keeping them together means one idea can run through all of them without being diluted at every hand-off.

Where is 13th Pencil based?

In Bangalore (Bengaluru), Karnataka. We work with brands wherever they are; one of our current clients is in Visakhapatnam.

Does 13th Pencil use AI?

Yes, every day, for exploring and producing. We do not let it make the final decision, because the decision is the part clients are paying for.
