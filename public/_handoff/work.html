Case Healthcare brand identity

Care for every stage of you.

Client
Dr Manju’s Women Health Centre, Visakhapatnam
Scope
Brand identity, logo system, colour palette, applications
Status
Identity approved, final revisions in progress
Credit
Concept and design, 13th Pencil, Bangalore

The brief. A gynaecology and women’s health practice was moving into a larger facility and needed a healthcare brand identity to match: calm and personal to a first-time patient, carrying professional weight, and working across a clinic sign, a prescription pad and a phone screen alike.

The approach. After an initial exploration, two logo directions were developed within one warm colour system, both centred on the bond between mother and child. We lead with Her Circle, which tells care as a circle that runs through every stage of a woman’s life.

Lead direction

Her Circle

A complete circle of care, connecting womanhood, motherhood and the journey through every stage.

Continuity · Motherhood · Complete care

Why it leads

  1. It is the line.A circle has no start and no end, which is exactly the promise in “care for every stage of you”. The mark says it before the words do.
  2. It covers a whole life.It reads as a woman’s journey rather than a single moment, which suits a centre whose care reaches well beyond pregnancy.
  3. It steps around the cliché.The heart is the most familiar shape in healthcare, and the one patients already link with cardiology. The circle is ownable.
  4. It holds up small.A closed silhouette survives one colour, a reversed background, a favicon and a prescription stamp without losing its shape.

Applications are shown for visualisation. Final executions are developed around the selected identity.

• In plain words

Brand identity, packaging and website design, in plain words.

13th Pencil is a creative and innovation company based in Bangalore, and this page shows the brand identity work we are building right now, for real businesses, at the stage it is actually at.

Beardman Company is a beard grooming and biker apparel brand. We are shaping its brand identity, packaging design and ecommerce website as one system, so the product looks like itself on the shelf, in the photograph and at the checkout, and stands apart from the black bottles that crowd the category.

Questions people ask

What kind of work does 13th Pencil do?

Brand strategy and positioning, brand identity and logo design, packaging design, art direction, film and content, AI-assisted creative production, and brand experiences for launches, events, retail and spaces. Most projects combine several of these, which is why they sit under one roof.

Do you design brand identities for healthcare clinics and hospitals?

Yes. Dr Manju’s Women Health Centre is a current example: a logo system, colour palette and visual language designed to feel calm, warm and trustworthy to patients, and flexible enough to carry across signage, stationery and digital channels.

Do you only work with brands in Bangalore?

No. We are based in Bangalore and work with brands wherever they are. Dr Manju’s Women Health Centre, for example, is in Visakhapatnam.

Can you design packaging and an ecommerce website together?

That is the Beardman Company brief. Designing the packaging and the online store as one system keeps a new brand consistent everywhere a customer meets it, from the product photograph to the box that arrives at the door.

Why are there only two projects here?

Because we would rather show two real projects than borrow twelve. 13th Pencil is new, and some of our other work belongs to people who have not yet agreed to it being shown. When they do, it goes here.
