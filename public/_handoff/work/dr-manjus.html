Work  /  Case study · Healthcare brand identity

Care for every stage of you.

Dr Manju’s Women Health Centre grew on trust, one patient at a time. Moving into a bigger home, it needed a name on the building that reassures a nervous first-time patient before anyone says a word, and still carries a doctor’s authority.

Client
Dr Manju’s Women Health Centre
Scope
Brand identity, logo system, colour
Status
Approved, final artwork in progress

The work

01 / 08
01

We walked past the heart.

It is on every hospital and insurance card, and patients read it as cardiology.

02

The idea is a circle.

A mother cradles her child inside a circle with no beginning and no end: care for every stage.

03

The name beside it.

A calm serif and the line it all answers to.

04

Warmth with authority.

Deep plum, rose, a touch of gold, and ivory in place of stark white.

05

Made to work small.

One colour, reversed, or the size of a fingertip.

06

At the front desk.

The first thing she sees inside.

07

In her hands.

The prescription she takes home.

08

On the doctor who listens.

Two directions, one decision.

Both directions went all the way to the building, in the same palette. Drag to see the one the centre did not choose.

Where it stands.

Approved. The final artwork is being drawn now, with every file the centre needs for its new home.

  1. Master logo artwork
  2. Logo variations
  3. Colour and type specs
  4. Print and digital files
  5. Usage guidelines
  6. Signage and stationery

Signage, reception, stationery and uniform images are visualisations of the approved identity.
