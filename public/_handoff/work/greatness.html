13th Pencil Originals  /  Film 02

Greatness doesn’t happen at ordinary speed.

A study in pace. One line, six shots and a cut that behaves like a race: the calm before the lights go out, then everything at once.

Type
Self-initiated film
Genre
Motorsport
Made with
Generative AI video
Shots
Six
Length
0:27, plus a vertical cut
01

One line, not a script.

The film started with a single thought: greatness doesn’t happen at ordinary speed. Every shot had to prove it, and the line itself appears in the film only once, at the very end.

02

Six shots, one car.

A shot list built like a race weekend: the helmet, the cockpit, the driver, then the track. One car and one livery, black with a coral stripe, held the same in every shot. No logos, no sponsors, nothing borrowed.

03

Cut like a race.

The raw shots looked beautiful but slow, while the engine sounded flat out. So the action shots run at up to twice speed, with frames blended for real motion blur and a little camera shake. The helmet and the eyes stay at normal speed. The calm is what makes the speed feel fast.

04

Two screens, two cuts.

One widescreen film for the website and one vertical cut for Reels and Shorts, each framed for its own screen, so nothing important is lost at the edges.

What this means for you.

Anything that should feel fast, without a shoot day, a track or a crew. The same method works for a product that needs to look its best in motion.

  • Product launches
  • Automotive and mobility
  • Sport and events
  • Tech and gadgets
  • Social-first films

Greatness doesn’t happen at ordinary speed is a self-initiated film made by 13th Pencil, not for a client. The car and livery are invented.
