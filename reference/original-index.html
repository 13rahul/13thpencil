How most of this industry introduces itself

We help brands thrive in a rapidly changing world. We start where the obvious answer stops.

13th Pencil is a creative and innovation company. We work on brands, campaigns, products and experiences — usually at the point where the expected route has been mapped and nobody is excited by it.

The expected answer is useful. It tells you where the edge of the category is. It is a starting position, not a strategy, and it is rarely the thing anyone remembers.

Strategy, creative, AI, experiences and growth — held together by one team rather than passed between five.

Twelve is a pattern.
Thirteen is a decision.

A pattern is what everyone can already see. It is agreement, arriving early and dressed as insight.

The departure comes later. It breaks the alignment, it is harder to defend in a meeting, and it is usually the only part anyone repeats afterwards. A pencil is the right tool for that moment — it sketches, questions, erases and commits, in that order.

How the line gets drawn

Step one

Find the tension

Interrogate the category, the customer and the thing nobody in the business wants to say out loud.

Step two

Exhaust the expected

Every reasonable route goes on the wall, argued properly, before anyone is allowed a favourite.

Step three

Clear the wall

Those routes were the map. They show where the category ends, which is the only place worth working.

And then

Make the departure

One idea gets built properly — film, identity, product, experience — with the craft it was worth in the first place.
